Ohm's Law

V = I × R
Voltage equals current multiplied by resistance. Related power formulas include P = V × I, P = I² × R, and P = V² ÷ R.

Ohm's Law formulas

Ohm's Law describes the relationship between voltage, current, and resistance in a resistive electrical circuit. The fundamental relationship is V = I × R.

Calculate Formula Unit
Voltage V = I × R Volts (V)
Current I = V ÷ R Amperes (A)
Resistance R = V ÷ I Ohms (Ω)
Power P = V × I Watts (W)
Power P = I² × R Watts (W)
Power P = V² ÷ R Watts (W)

How to use this calculator

Enter at least two electrical quantities and click Calculate. For example, entering 120 V and 5 A produces 24 Ω of resistance and 600 W of power.

If you enter three or four quantities, EE Tools also checks whether those values agree with the Ohm's Law relationships.

This calculator is intended for resistive relationships. AC circuits containing significant inductance or capacitance may require impedance, phase angle, and power factor calculations.
