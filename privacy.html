Privacy Policy

This page explains how EE Tools handles information when you use our website and electrical engineering calculators.

Last updated: September 10, 2026

EE Tools calculators are designed to perform calculations directly in your web browser. Values entered into calculator input fields are not intentionally stored or transmitted by EE Tools unless a future feature clearly states otherwise.

Analytics

EE Tools may use website analytics services in the future to understand how visitors use the site, which calculators are most useful, and how the website can be improved.

If analytics services are added, this Privacy Policy will be updated to identify relevant services and data practices.

EE Tools does not currently require user accounts or intentionally use cookies for calculator functionality.

Third-party services such as analytics or advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies if those services are added to the website in the future.

Advertising

EE Tools may display advertisements in the future. Advertising providers may collect certain information according to their own privacy policies and applicable law.

EE Tools may contain links to third-party websites, references, products, or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

Children's privacy

EE Tools is intended as a general educational and professional engineering resource and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to operate the website securely. However, no website or internet transmission method can guarantee complete security.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as EE Tools adds new functionality, analytics, advertising, or other services. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the contact information provided on the EE Tools Contact page.
