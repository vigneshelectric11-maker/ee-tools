Current Formula

I = P ÷ V

For DC systems, current equals power divided by voltage.

Current Calculation Formulas

Electrical current can be calculated from real power and voltage. AC calculations also require the load power factor.

System Current Formula
DC I = P ÷ V
Single-Phase AC I = P ÷ (V × PF)
Three-Phase AC I = P ÷ (√3 × VLL × PF)

How to Calculate Amps from Watts

For a DC circuit, divide power in watts by voltage. For example, a 1,200 W DC load operating at 120 V draws:

I = 1200 ÷ 120 = 10 A

AC systems require power factor because voltage and current may not be in phase. For balanced three-phase systems, the formula also includes the square root of three.

Example Calculations

Single-phase: A 2.4 kW load operating at 240 V with a power factor of 0.80 draws 12.5 A.

Three-phase: A 100 kW balanced three-phase load operating at 480 V with a power factor of 0.90 draws approximately 133.65 A.

Engineering Note

This calculator provides current estimates from real power, voltage, and power factor. AC calculations assume sinusoidal steady-state quantities, and the three-phase calculation assumes a balanced system using line-to-line voltage and line current.

Calculated operating current should not automatically be treated as conductor ampacity, protective-device rating, motor nameplate current, or code-table full-load current. Verify actual design requirements using applicable standards, equipment data, and qualified engineering judgment.
